Privacy Policy

Effective April 16, 2026 · Last updated April 16, 2026
01 —

Who We Are

Grynd ("we", "our", "us") is a behavioral enforcement platform built for competitive exam students in India. We operate the Grynd web application at grynd.tech and the GryndMode Chrome Extension.

We are committed to handling your data transparently. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used.

02 —

Information We Collect

We collect two categories of data — account information provided during sign-in, and behavioral data generated through your use of the platform and extension.

Name
Collected via Google OAuth at account creation. Used to personalise your dashboard.
Email Address
Collected via Google OAuth. Used for authentication, account recovery, and product communications.
Study Sessions
Session start/end times, subject, mode (Pomodoro / Custom), focus duration, and interruption events.
Behavioral Events
Tab activity, URL categories, active vs idle time, and distraction patterns — captured by the GryndMode Chrome Extension while a study session is active.
Derived Metrics
Focus Score, Consistency Score, and Ideal vs Actual adherence — computed from your session data.

The GryndMode extension only tracks browser activity during active study sessions. Tracking is strictly session-scoped. We do not monitor general browsing outside of an active Grynd session.

03 —

How We Use Your Data

All data collected is used solely to deliver and improve the Grynd product experience. Specifically:

authentication Sign-in and identity verification via Google OAuth.
enforcement Detecting distraction patterns and generating behavioral nudges during sessions.
analytics Computing your focus metrics, streaks, and performance trends shown on your dashboard.
product improvement Aggregate, anonymised usage data to improve platform features.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

04 —

Google OAuth & Third-Party Services

We use Google OAuth 2.0 for authentication. We only request access to your name and email address — we do not access your Google Calendar, Gmail, Drive, or any other Google services.

Our infrastructure runs on Supabase (database and authentication) hosted on secure cloud servers. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.

05 —

GryndMode Chrome Extension

The GryndMode extension monitors your active browser tab and URL patterns exclusively within an active Grynd study session. It classifies activity into study or distraction categories and surfaces behavioral nudges to keep you on task.

The extension does not:

✕ Read page content or form inputs.
✕ Track activity outside of active Grynd sessions.
✕ Access passwords, payment info, or personal files.
✕ Share data with any entity outside of Grynd.

06 —

Data Retention

We retain your account data and session history for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all personal data and session records are permanently deleted within 30 days.

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at the address below.

07 —

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise any of these rights, email us directly. We will respond within 7 business days.
